❓ Help Center

Everything you need to use Sushi Cafe

🍣 Getting Started +
  • Add Sushi Cafe as a friend on LINE
  • Tap Register and fill in your details
  • Use the Menu to start ordering
  • Earn points automatically when you order
🛒 How to Order +
  • Open the Menu from the Rich Menu
  • Select your sushi items
  • Confirm your order
  • Wait for the shop to prepare your order
💎 Points & Rewards +
  • You earn 1 point for every $10 spent
  • Check your points in My Points
  • Points update after order completion
👤 My Profile +
  • View your phone number and email
  • Check your role and points
  • Keep your information up to date
❓ Common Questions +
  • I don’t see the menu – Try reopening the chat
  • Points not updated – Only completed orders count
  • Order not confirmed – Please wait a moment
Need more help? Use the Contact button in the menu.
